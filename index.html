01 / About

The person behind the plumbing.

I'm a Marketing Operations Executive at Ascendion, and the only HubSpot admin for a global marketing team spread across North America, India and EMEA.

My job is to keep marketing running: CRM and forms, campaign execution, global events, vendors and budgets. I also run account-based marketing for 15 enterprise client accounts across APAC, North America and Europe, working directly with clients and the Markets team.

Before this, I spent over two years as Marketing Analyst for APAC at Horizontal Talent.

These days a big part of my work is AI. I build automations in n8n, write small apps in Python, and use LLMs to turn slow manual work into something that runs on its own.

Outside work, I shoot and edit automotive photography, and I go back to my business school to talk to new MBA students about what marketing really looks like today.
